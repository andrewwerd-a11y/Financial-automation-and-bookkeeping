# Vision

Self-employed people juggle a dozen apps. Each one holds part of their reputation, part of their income, and part of their schedule, and none of them talk to each other. Small businesses and crews have the opposite problem: they need trustworthy help now, and they can't see who is qualified, available, and nearby.

Polymathic is the **information broker and general contractor** between them.

## Pillars

1. **Aggregate every source of work.** Gig marketplaces, local-service apps, job boards, staffing agencies, government postings, and Polymathic's own contracts all go into one feed, normalized and de-duplicated.
2. **Honest comparison.** Every job is converted to *effective hourly take-home pay*, after platform fees, vehicle cost, and drive time. A $600 job two hours away and a $28/hr job next door can be compared directly.
3. **Portable trust.** Track records imported from every connected platform, plus identity, background, insurance, and certification checks, roll up into one explainable trust score. Workers can always see why they're at their tier and what moves them up.
4. **Immediate and long-term work.** An "available now" toggle surfaces same-day work. Scheduled, contract, temp, and occasional full-time roles sit in the same feed.
5. **Forward momentum.** The growth planner looks at real demand in the worker's area and says which certification, equipment, or skill would unlock the most work, and how much it's worth.
6. **Polymathic as general contractor.** Businesses post jobs to Polymathic, and Polymathic staffs them with vetted subcontractors (or crews). It handles escrow, payment, insurance, and dispute resolution.
7. **Safety & verification.** Identity checks, background checks where the law allows, masked communication, job check-ins, SOS, and consented job-site documentation.
8. **Recordkeeping.** Earnings from every platform, expenses, mileage, 1099s, invoices, and contracts, all in one dashboard and exportable to accounting tools.

## Later expansions

- Business marketplace, storefronts, and domain hosting for independents
- Cross-platform shopping comparison and market research for tools and supplies
- The resale AI / cross-listing system and the garage & estate sale listing and sourcing tools, joined into the same identity, trust, and payments backbone

## Principles

- **Explainable over magical.** Every score and ranking shows how it was calculated.
- **Worker-owned data.** Connections are consented, revocable, and exportable.
- **One broken integration never breaks the product.** Every source is optional.
